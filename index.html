Veylo Open Veylo

A quiet board for the work in front of you.

Veylo is a kanban app that runs in your browser. Make as many boards as you need, add columns, tags, subtasks and due dates, and get on with it. There is nothing to sign up for.

Open Veylo Free. No account. Your boards stay on your device.
Website relaunch

Example cards. Click one to move it to the next column.

Everything a small board needs

Many boards, one sidebar

Give every project its own board and switch between them from the sidebar.

Columns, tags and subtasks

Rename and reorder columns, label cards with tags, and split work into subtasks with due dates.

Board or list

See the same cards as columns or as a grouped to-do list, and switch with one click.

Search and shortcuts

Press Ctrl K or ⌘K to find any card, board or action. C adds a card and V switches the view.

Your boards stay on your device

Veylo saves everything in your browser’s own storage. Nothing is uploaded and the site sets no cookies. That also means boards do not sync between devices, and clearing your browser’s site data erases them. Read the privacy policy for details.
